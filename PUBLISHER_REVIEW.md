# PUBLISHER_REVIEW.md

> A publisher's commercial evaluation of **Portfolio Puzzle** against a $500,000
> finishing investment. Engineering assumed excellent. The only question: **will
> people buy it?** Benchmarked against Balatro, Game Dev Story, Pocket City, Luck
> Be a Landlord, Papers Please, Mini Metro, Into the Breach.
> Changes marked ✅ shipped alongside this review.

---

## 1. Brutally Honest Commercial Review

**What you have:** a mechanically deep, charming, mobile-shaped tycoon with a
genuinely novel skill loop (interpreting ambiguous news), a freshly-installed
emotional layer (dreams, breakup texts, a corrupt politician), and a distinctive
diegetic frame (pixel desk / PC / phone). Sessions are 2–5 minutes. The systems
interact for real. This is *far* above the median pitch deck.

**What you don't have:** a replayability engine, enough content, any audio, a
marketable name, or a single screenshot that sells itself. Right now this is a
**brilliant 4-hour game** wearing the price tag of a 40-hour one.

**Where it loses against the comp set:**
- **vs Balatro / Luck Be a Landlord:** no run structure. One endless career; death
  at week 34 erases everything and offers nothing — no unlocks, no seeds, no "one
  more run." Those games are retention machines *because* of runs + meta-unlocks.
  This is the single largest commercial gap.
- **vs Game Dev Story:** content breadth. Kairosoft ships dozens of staff, genres,
  consoles, events. You have 4 clients, ~36 headlines, 12 scoops, 5 crash flavors.
  Players will see substantially everything in one evening.
- **vs Mini Metro:** screenshot identity. Mini Metro is a poster. Your screens are
  text tables in boxes; only the desk scene and the pixel faces are ownable.
- **vs Papers Please:** you finally have moral texture (insider trading, Bill,
  ruined dreams) but it's under-exploited — no consequences, no dilemmas, no press
  angle yet.
- **Everything vs everything:** silence. No audio at all. A trailer without an
  audio identity is dead on arrival in 2026.

**Where it wins:**
- The **news-interpretation skill loop** is genuinely fresh — nobody in the casual
  tycoon space makes "reading comprehension under uncertainty" the core verb.
- The **emotional stakes now exist** and produce shareable sentences: *"I
  bankrupted a 24-year-old's food-truck dream via insider trading"* is a TikTok
  clip, a Steam review, and a streamer moment in one line.
- The **fantasy reframed correctly is universal**: not "portfolio management"
  (homework) but *morally flexible Wall Street hustle* (Wolf of Wall Street ×
  Game Dev Story). Marketing must lead with greed, crashes, and corruption — never
  with the word "portfolio."
- **Mobile-shaped by construction:** turn-based weeks, portrait, offline, no
  timers. Premium mobile is a credible lane (Pocket City proved it).
- **The name must change.** "Portfolio Puzzle" is SEO-generic, sounds like an
  edu-app, and misdescribes the game (it hasn't been a puzzle for 30 commits).
  This needs the developer's call — candidates that sell the actual fantasy:
  *BULL RUN*, *GOOD ADVICE.*, *INSIDER*, *THE STREET*, *FIDUCIARY!*.

## 2. SWOT

- **Strengths:** interacting systems depth · novel core verb · fresh emotional
  layer · diegetic charm · 2-min sessions · zero-dependency tech = low burn ·
  docs/discipline of a much bigger team.
- **Weaknesses:** no run/meta structure · demo-sized content pool · no audio ·
  weak screenshots · generic name · single game mode · finance theme reads
  "homework" until reframed.
- **Opportunities:** "immoral finance" press hook (Papers-Please adjacency) ·
  daily seeded challenge = streamer/leaderboard lane · premium mobile is starved
  for depth · finance-curious zeitgeist (r/wallstreetbets audience never gets
  games made for it) · cross-promo demo via existing web build.
- **Threats:** Balatro-class competition for the "one more run" dollar · content
  treadmill vs small team velocity · store featuring lottery · theme
  misperception ("is this a budgeting app?") killing click-through.

## 3. Would I fund it?

**Yes — conditionally, in tranches.** Not $500k on day one. **$150k now** against
a 90-day milestone: run-structured build + audio pass + 3× content + renamed
store page + public demo, gated on **10,000 Steam wishlists in 60 days of page
live**. Hit that and the remaining **$350k** funds the 12-month plan below. The
team's demonstrated velocity (this codebase) is the reason I'm in at all.

- **What excites me:** the emotional clip-ability; the untouched niche; the
  session shape; the discipline. **What scares me:** the content ceiling; run
  structure arriving late; solo content velocity; the name; silence.
- **Must improve before launch (non-negotiable):** run/season structure with
  meta-unlocks · audio identity · content ×3 (12 clients, 100+ headlines, event
  chains) · rename + store art · difficulty modes + daily seed.

## 5. Risks / Opportunities (biggest of each)

**Risks:** content ceiling discovered by reviewers ("saw everything in 3 hours")
· run-structure retrofit destabilizing balance · theme misread as edu-app ·
silence at trailer time · single-dev bus factor.
**Opportunities:** own an empty niche ("the finance roguelite") · daily-seed
streamer culture · the Bill/insider angle as earned press · wallstreetbets-adjacent
virality · premium mobile scarcity of depth.

## 6. Roadmaps

**90 days (tranche-1 gate):** freeze new systems ✅ · season structure + meta
unlock skeleton · audio pass 1 · content to 8 clients / 70 headlines · rename +
capsule + Steam page live · web demo with wishlist CTA · daily seed prototype ·
unified reveal + stamp juice. *Gate: 10k wishlists.*

**6 months:** content to 12 clients/100+ headlines/12 swans · insider-scandal
system · political tier · scenario mode · Next Fest demo · 3 creator previews ·
achievements · localization pass 1 · Steam launch ($9.99–12.99) targeting a
quiet window.

**12 months:** mobile premium launch ($4.99, one SKU, no IAP) · post-launch
content drop 1 (new tier + scenarios) · daily-challenge live-ops cadence ·
localization pass 2 · evaluate Switch port (the natural home).

**Feature freeze (now):** no new economic mechanics. Everything ships into
content, structure, juice, audio, and store presence. The sim is done; the
*game around the sim* is the remaining work.

**Polish priorities:** week reveal > report card > phone texts > desk scene >
shop. (In that order — polish where the emotion already lives.)

## 7. Marketing / Store / Creators / Retention

- **Positioning line:** *"A tiny, morally flexible Wall Street. Read the news
  before it happens. Fund their dreams — or gamble them away."* Never say
  "portfolio," "educational," or "simulator" above the fold.
- **Steam page:** capsule = sad pixel client + red chart behind the desk; first
  screenshot = breakup text; second = CONTRACT COMPLETE S-grade; third = black
  swan aftermath; GIF of the reveal. Tags: Simulation, Tycoon, Pixel Graphics,
  Roguelite (post-seasons), Capitalism (yes, it's a real tag and it converts).
- **Mobile stores:** premium $4.99, "no ads · no IAP · offline" as literal store
  bullets; feature pitch to editorial via the Papers-Please-of-finance angle.
- **Wishlist strategy:** page live at rename → demo on itch + web → 3 devlogs
  (the "I made insider trading a game mechanic" post is the banker) → Next Fest.
- **Creators:** seed 20 mid-size tycoon/roguelite streamers with a build + a
  personalized client named after them (cheap, irresistible). Daily seed gives
  them recurring content. TikTok/Shorts: 15-second breakup-text reveals.
- **Retention:** seasons + dailies + records (✅ started) + content drops.
  D1/D7/D30 targets post-structure: 45/20/8 (premium mobile norms).

## 8. Predictions & Revenue Scenarios (honest ranges)

- **Review scores:** Steam 78–84% positive at current content (capped by "not
  enough game"); **86–92%** with the 6-month plan executed. Mobile 4.3–4.6.
- **Downloads (first year):** Floor: 3k Steam / 8k mobile (missed featuring, no
  virality). Base: 20k Steam / 60k mobile (one featuring, modest creator pickup).
  Breakout: 150k+ Steam / 500k+ mobile (daily-seed streamer adoption — the
  Balatro lottery; do not budget on it).
- **Revenue @ $9.99 Steam / $4.99 mobile (net ~65%):** Floor ≈ $45k · Base ≈
  **$325k** · Breakout ≈ $2.5M+. Base case pays back $500k only with the mobile
  tail and a content drop — hence the tranche structure. As a $150k first
  tranche: comfortably rational.

   REVIEW** card — average weekly return, firm income, dreams funded, contracts
   completed, best week — and awards a **career title** (Associate → Advisor →

   contracts completed, S-grades — tracked in state, celebrated with a **★ NEW
   RECORD** beat on the week reveal, listed in the Shop's FINANCES tab and the
   game-over screen.
3. **Trophy shelf:** funded dreams appear as trophies on the desk — the iconic
   screenshot frame now displays your career's proudest artifacts.
4. **First-impression fixes:** README rewritten as a real game pitch (the old one
   described a deleted prototype); app.json splash/theme aligned to the current
   palette. (Rename itself left to the developer — see §1.)
